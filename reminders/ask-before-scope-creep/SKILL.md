---
name: ask-before-scope-creep
title: Ask Before Scope Creep
description: Confirm before large refactors, new dependencies, or work beyond what was asked.
---

Do what was asked. Before a broad refactor, a new runtime dependency, a file-layout change,
or any work meaningfully larger than the request, stop and confirm — describe what you would
do and why, and let the user decide.

Noticing an unrelated problem is useful; fixing it uninvited is not. Report it and move on.
